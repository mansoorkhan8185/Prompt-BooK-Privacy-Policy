Privacy Policy - Prompt Book

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Prompt Book ("the app") shows a library of text prompts that you can copy or open in other apps. This policy explains what information is involved when you use it.

Information we do not collect

The app has no accounts or sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

Information stored on your device

Your favourite prompts and the last downloaded list of prompts are saved on your device so the app works faster and offline. You can remove them by clearing the app's data in your phone settings.

Content from our server

The app downloads prompts and settings from a Google Firebase Realtime Database, and prompt images from the internet. These requests may reveal your IP address to those servers, as with any internet connection.

Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob and Unity Ads. These services may collect and use device information such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and app usage to show and measure ads, and may show personalized ads where permitted. In the European Economic Area and the UK, you are asked for consent where required. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Unity Ads: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Links to other apps

When you tap ChatGPT or Claude, the prompt text is sent to that service's website or app. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
